Entrants write a single sentence, at most 200 characters, that explains the modern internet to a time traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800. The sentence has to stand alone: no words coined after the traveler's time, no list of features, just a picture they could actually hold in their head.

## The sentence

> It is a road joining every library and post office in the world, so letters cross the earth in a breath and books from libraries far away can be read from your own chair.

One road, two things it does: letters arrive in a breath, and the books of far libraries open in your own chair. Every word in it is a word a person from 1800 already knew.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, no trailing newline). Python 3.13, stdlib only, no network, deterministic. The program self-checks before writing: the text must be one sentence of 1–200 characters.
